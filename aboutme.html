Meet Chloe Ray

The passion and heart behind Dance With Chloe

My Story

My name is Chloe Ray, and my love for dance started at just four years old. At eight, I began competitive dancing, and at fourteen I began teaching students and choreographing dances. Through my many years of dancing, I discovered my true passion fo teaching and inspiring young dancers.

After I graduated highschool, I knew I wanted to pursue a career in teaching and choerographing dance, while creating something of my very own. So, "Dance With Chloe" was formally founded August of 2026. Dance With Chloe is an extension of myself, showcasing my love of dance and art, while creating a positive, encouraging environment for new dancers.

My Philosophy

When it comes to teaching, I strive to encourage, build confidene, and create connection. I make choreography that highlights each child's unique strengths, while helping them grow as dancers and individuals. Every child has a story to tell; the greatest joy in life is being able to tell that story with the beauty of dance.

Experience & Training

  • Competitive Dancer for 10 Years
  • Choregraphing and Teaching Dance for 4 Years
