What is Dance With Chloe?

Dance With Chloe was established in August of 2026, by Chloe Ray, with the goal of making dance accessible and cost-effective for everyone. At Dance With Chloe, dancers are empowered and supported, while learning to build self confidence one step at a time.

I offer both competitive and recreational dance opportunities. Whether you're interested in competing or dancing simply for fun, I offer competition solos, duets, trios, group classes, private lessons, team routines, and so much more. I work with my dancers to build condifence, develop skills, learn to enjoy the art of dance, and most of all, have fun!

As a mobile dance studio, I do not have a permanent building. Instead, I have a space available for lessons or I can bring dance directly to you. This flexibility allows dancers and families to choose what works best for their schedule and needs.
